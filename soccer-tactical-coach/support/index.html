Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. The app opens straight onto the board. There is nothing to sign up for.

Does it work without a signal?
Yes, always. The app does not connect to the internet at all, so it behaves identically on a touchline with no reception.

Where are my boards stored?
On your device, and nowhere else. They are not backed up to any server, because there is no server. Deleting the app deletes them.

Why are my players called “Unnamed”?
Both squads start as numbers and positions only, so nothing is invented for you. Open Edit Squads and put your own players in.

Can I undo a mistake?
Yes. Undo and redo cover every action on the board, and the squad screen has its own undo.

Reporting a problem

If something is wrong, the two things that help most are what you were doing when it happened, and which device you were on. A screenshot is worth more than either.
