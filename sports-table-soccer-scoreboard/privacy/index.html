Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Sports Table Soccer Scoreboard does not collect any data.

Everything you enter in the app — team and player names, scores, colours and settings — is stored only on your own device, in the app’s own storage, so a match is not lost if the app closes. None of it is sent to us.

In plain terms:

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. The app contains no third-party SDKs of any kind, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • No network use. The app does not connect to the internet. It works the same at a venue with no signal as it does anywhere else.
  • No access to your photos, camera, microphone, contacts or location. The app never asks, because it never needs to.

Exporting, sharing and printing

You can export a match as a CSV file or a PDF result sheet, or print one. That only happens when you choose to, and the file goes only where you send it, using the share sheet or printer you pick. We never receive it. Once you have sent a file somewhere, it is handled by whichever app or service you sent it to.

Importing a CSV file reads it into the app on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Because nothing leaves your device unless you send it, there is nothing for us to see, share, sell or lose. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.
